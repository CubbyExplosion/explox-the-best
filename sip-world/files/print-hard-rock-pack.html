How to play along

1. Press play on the with clicks song. You hear 4 clicks (the count-in).

2. Start on the first click after the count-in. The piano sheet matches the recording bar for bar: bar 1 on the sheet is bar 1 in the song.

3. Play the sheet once (32 bars), then play it again for round 2. The band plays it twice.

4. The drum sheet matches too: every bar is the same bar number as the piano sheet. Follow the drum sheet to see where the fills and crashes land.

5. When you are ready, use the no clicks song.

Tip: Your right hand plays the tune. Your left hand plays the bass notes and chords. The guitar and bass in the recording play the left hand, so your left hand can double them.
