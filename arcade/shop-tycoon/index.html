❓ How to play

  • Customers walk in and ask for an item. Click the customer (or press Space) to sell it.
  • Open Stock and buy items wholesale with SIP. No stock, no sale!
  • Set your price in the Shop tab. Too high and customers walk away, too low and you earn little.
  • Hire cashiers (auto-sell), stockers (auto-restock), greeters and managers.
  • Expand the shop, buy ads and upgrades. Watch for daily events and the seasons.
  • You choose the price of every item. Customers compare it to the market price. Watch rent, wages, power and tax in the Report tab.
  • Each shop can earn at most 1000 SIP/s (base). To earn more, open branches in new streets from the bar above the shop. Franchise multipliers are applied after the cap.
  • Go Franchise for permanent bonuses once you have sold enough.
  • Goal: 1,000,000 items sold. Your shop keeps working while you are away (up to 8 hours).
  • Keys: Space = serve, P / Esc = pause, M = mute.
