⚡ The short version (for kids)

  • Be kind. No bullying, mean words, cheating, or hacking. Everyone online is a real person.
  • Keep your password secret and never tell anyone your real name, address, phone number, school, or email in chat.
  • S.I.P. and Elite are game money — not real money. They can't be turned into cash.
  • You can lose stuff in the game (like things you're carrying when you get knocked out). That's part of playing.
  • Buying things with real money is optional and needs a parent or another adult to say yes.
  • Explox shows ads. Some you can choose to watch to earn S.I.P.
  • We can remove people who break the rules, and we may change or fix the game as it grows.

If you're under 13, please read this page with a parent or guardian. Playing means you agree to the full rules below.

2

Who can play

Explox is made for kids and families, and anyone can play it for free.

  • If you are under 13, a parent or guardian should read these Terms with you before you make an account, and should know what you're playing.
  • Real-money purchases must only be made by an adult, or by a young person with a parent or guardian's clear permission and help. If you're not sure, ask first.
  • We don't ask for your real name, email address, home address or phone number to play. Please keep it that way — see Your information.
3

Your account

You can play Offline (your progress is saved only in that one browser) or Online with an account (your progress is saved on our server, and you share the world with other players). An account is just a username and a password.

  • Keep your password private. Never share it — not even with a friend or someone who says they work for Explox. We will never ask you for it.
  • You're responsible for what happens on your account. If you think someone else is using it, tell us right away.
  • Pick a username that's friendly and doesn't pretend to be someone else, a real person, or the Explox team. We can change or remove names that aren't okay.
  • Your account belongs to you alone. Please don't sell, trade, or give away accounts.
  • If you forget your password, we may not be able to get your progress back, so choose something you'll remember.

Playing fair & being kind

Explox only works if everyone plays nicely. You agree not to:

  • bully, threaten, harass, or be hateful or cruel to anyone, in chat, in your profile, or in the game world;
  • post anything rude, scary, or not okay for kids, or anything that shares someone's private information (including your own);
  • cheat, hack, use bugs or glitches to get things you shouldn't, or use tools that change how the game works;
  • try to break into other people's accounts, or to break, overload, or attack the game, its server, or this website;
  • trick other players into giving you things, or pretend to be an admin or a member of the Explox team;
  • use another person's real name or picture, or a real celebrity or politician's, in a way that could mislead people or hurt them.

What happens if rules are broken: we (and admins we trust) can delete messages, mute or warn people, take back items or S.I.P. that were gained unfairly, and pause or end accounts. We try to be fair, but we decide when a rule has been broken.

See something wrong?If someone is being unkind, or you find a bug, tell us instead of using it. Reporting a bug honestly is always better than taking advantage of it.

S.I.P., Elite & in-game items

S.I.P. and Elite are the game's pretend money, and everything you collect — cars, houses, outfits, weapons, armor, furniture, pets — is a game item. All of it:

  • is for use inside Explox only, and has no real-world value;
  • can't be cashed out, sold for real money, or traded for real money, gift cards, or anything outside the game;
  • is a licence to use the item in the game, not something you own like a physical object;
  • may be changed, re-balanced, repriced, reset, or removed when we update the game.

Losing things is part of the game. For example, if you get knocked out you can lose the items you're carrying (your bank, safe, houses, cars and outfits are kept). Games also have bugs and updates. We try hard to keep your progress safe, but we can't promise nothing will ever be lost, and losing in-game things is not a reason for a refund.

Real-money purchases (optional)

Explox is free to play. You can choose to buy extras with real money, such as packs of S.I.P. or Elite, special items (like premium vehicles or armor), and rentals. You never have to buy anything to play.

  • Who can buy: adults, or young people with a parent or guardian's permission (see Who can play).
  • How you pay: payments are handled by Stripe, a payment company. You type your payment details on Stripe's secure checkout, not into Explox — we never see or keep your card number.
  • Prices are shown in US dollars at checkout before you pay. Taxes may be added where the law requires it.
  • What you get: purchased S.I.P./Elite and items are added to your account right after payment. They're game items, so section 5 applies to them too.
  • Rentals (like renting a vehicle) are subscriptions that renew until you cancel. You can cancel any time. When a rental ends, you lose access to the rented item.
  • Problems: if you were charged but didn't receive what you bought, contact us (see Changes & contact) and we'll fix it or refund you.

Ads

Explox is free partly because it shows ads, provided by Google. Some ads are optional — for example, you can press a button to watch an ad and earn S.I.P. Other ads can appear at certain moments, like after you respawn.

  • We don't choose or control the ads themselves, and we're not responsible for advertisers or the websites they link to.
  • Never share personal information with an ad, and ask a grown-up before you click anything that leaves the game.
  • If you use an ad blocker, some features that need ads (like the optional "watch an ad" reward) may not work.
8

Other players & shared features

In Online mode you share the world with real people. Chat is seen by everyone online, and you can send S.I.P., hire other players for jobs, visit and shop at other players' stores, and see leaderboards.

  • Everything players trade, pay, or hire each other for happens in game money only. Please never trade real money, gift cards, or accounts for game items — we can't help if that goes wrong.
  • Be honest in trades and jobs. Scams and tricks aren't allowed (see Playing fair), and we can undo trades that were unfair or caused by a bug.
  • Other players aren't part of the Explox team. We're not responsible for what they say or do, so be careful, and tell us about anyone who makes you uncomfortable.
  • Your name and some game stats (like your S.I.P. and records) can appear on public leaderboards and profiles.

Your information

We keep this as small as we can. Here is what Explox handles:

  • Your account: your username and a password that is scrambled in your browser before it's sent to us, plus your game progress and the dates you joined and last played.
  • What you share in the game: chat messages (the most recent ones are kept so everyone can catch up), your profile and bio, and game stats that may show on leaderboards.
  • Offline play: saved only in your own browser. Clearing your browser's site data erases it.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We receive confirmation of what you bought, not your card details.
  • Ads: Google shows the ads and may use cookies or similar identifiers to do it. See Google's own policies for how they work.
  • Ratings and comments left on our home page are public, so don't put anything private in them.
  • Services we rely on (to host the website, run the game server, store accounts, take payments, and show ads) handle data for us. We don't sell your information.
