👋 About us

Who makes these games, and why.

I'm Cubby Explosion, and our team is S.I.P. (Super Important Projects). We make free games that run right in your web browser — no download, no installing, and they work on computers and phones. Everything here is built one feature at a time, with players' ideas in mind.

On Explox, I decide what the game needs next and play-test it, Hubby helps with the code and the sound, and gurnaldst made the real soundtracks. City Life, Block Obby and the mini games come from the same S.I.P. team.
