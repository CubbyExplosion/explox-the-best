What you can do

An entire life, built to explore

Explox isn't a level you finish — it's a city you live in. Everything below is real and playable today.

🏪

Own & run a real store

Buy one of 10 store tiers, stock real shelves by physically carrying boxes, set your own prices, hire staff, and run ads to bring in customers.

🛍️

300 real shops to shop in

A whole Shopping District outdoors and a Shopping Wing inside the Mall — every shop has real items with real prices you can actually buy.

👋

40 named neighbors with real lives

Meet, befriend, invite over, and hire your Suburbs neighbors — each has their own house, car, and job. Marry two of them off and they'll start a family.

💼

Jobs that need real work

Clock in as a Shopkeeper or Officer and actually respond to tasks in time to get paid — wander off the job and you're auto-fired, just like real life.

✈️

Fly to 8 countries

Japan, France, Brazil, Egypt, the UK, Australia, Canada, and Italy — each with its own landmarks and a small town of themed shops to explore.

🎬

A real working movie theater

14 full movies with animated scenes, snacks you can actually eat, and a real trailer-to-credits cinema experience.
