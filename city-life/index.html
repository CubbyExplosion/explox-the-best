🏙️ City Life

A free 3D life simulator by Cubby Explosion and the S.I.P. team. Plays in your browser — no download, on phones and computers.

▶ Play City Life YouTube

What is City Life?

You live a whole life, from a baby to age 85, in a blocky 3D city. Pick a boy or a girl, choose where your family is from, go to school, make friends, get a job, study at university and move into your own place.

What you can do

  • 🏫 Go to school and answer questions, play at recess, and do homework
  • 🎓 Go to university and take hands-on lectures in 8 subjects
  • 💼 Work 17 different jobs, pay bills and decide what to buy
  • 🏠 Move out, buy furniture, and have Mom and Dad visit
  • 🍽️ Shop at the mall, eat at restaurants, cook and manage your fridge
  • 🌍 Choose from 40 countries for your family's skin tone, language and foods
  • 🗣️ People talk with over 100 different voices
  • 🐾 Look after a pet, make friends, date, and start a family
  • 📱 Use a phone, shop for electronics, and collect bugs in your Bug Guide
  • 🚗 Drive your own car from age 18 (watch out for the police!)

Good to know

City Life saves in your browser. It has flashing police lights and some screen shake, so it may not suit people with epilepsy or photosensitivity — you can turn camera shake off in Settings.
